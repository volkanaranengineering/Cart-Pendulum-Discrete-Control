# Dependencies and reuse

MATLAB and Simulink are MathWorks products and remain external dependencies. Their executables, toolboxes and installed runtime files are not distributed. The `.m` source and `.slx` models here are the study artifacts.

Optional tutorial generation uses ReportLab and Pillow, installed separately under their own licenses. PDF inspection uses external Poppler. Git LFS is needed to retrieve full-size archived datasets.

No NASA T-MATS files are included. No endorsement by MathWorks, NASA, or OpenAI is implied. No new blanket license is assigned to the research material; contact the repository owner regarding reuse permission.
